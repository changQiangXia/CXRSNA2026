# Competition Card

## Task

- **Competition:** RSNA Knee Abnormality Detection 2026
- **Type:** code competition, multilabel medical-image classification
- **Input at inference:** knee MRI DICOM studies only
- **Output:** one probability per study for 12 findings
- **Metric:** macro ROC AUC over the 12 independent columns
- **Submission:** `submission.csv` generated by a hidden notebook rerun
- **Runtime limit:** 9 hours

## Data shape

From the most detailed public write-ups and the solo inventory:

```text
train studies             4,407
train series             24,371
expert-labelled studies      58
test studies        approximately 1,300
reports in training         yes
reports at test              no
finding count                12
```

The 58 expert-labelled studies are often called **gold58**. They are useful for
spot-checking label quality and detecting gross regressions, but their paired
noise is too large to rank subtle model changes reliably.

## Hidden-test interpretation

The test studies do not contain reports. A report can be used as a teacher for
the training set, but it cannot be used as an inference feature. The deployed
model must be image-only.

## Why the task is hard

1. **Label scarcity:** only 58 expert-labelled training studies.
2. **Report leakage trap:** reports exist only in training, so they can teach
   but cannot be fed to the deployed model.
3. **Language and site variation:** reports are multilingual and hospital
   wording differs.
4. **Definition mismatch:** “small effusion” and low-grade MCL injuries may not
   satisfy the competition’s positive definition.
5. **Metric arithmetic:** each of the 12 findings contributes equally.
6. **Hidden runtime:** an ensemble that is powerful locally but exceeds nine
   hours receives no score.
