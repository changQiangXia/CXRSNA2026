# Attributions

This archive is a summary and provenance record. It does not redistribute the
raw competition data, model weights, or full third-party write-ups.

Important public contributors and resources cited during the competition and in
the final retrospective include:

- **tascj0, liushuzhi and Team Kangaroo** — agentic labeling, ROI/lesion boxes,
  anatomy-aware models, and first-place solution.
- **sakaku** — Astra labels, anatomical boxes, and the paired first-place
  write-up.
- **azamat1ch and MURAD** — second-place multi-reader solution.
- **DataTech Club (Zhihong Li et al.)** — third-place grouped probability blend
  and external OAI supervision.
- **BarryZhou (songqizhou)** — seventh-place, competition-data-only solution.
- **matsurih** — solo-gold solution and detailed validation/ablation write-up.
- **Komil Parmar** — solo silver solution and the per-finding probe idea.
- **goodpjw2008**, **heliosli**, **nartaa**, **varshithakolupuri**, and the
  broader Kaggle community for public readers, checkpoints, and blend code.
- **RSNA, the annotating radiologists, contributing institutions, and Kaggle**
  for the dataset and competition infrastructure.
