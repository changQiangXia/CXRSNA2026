# Public Intelligence

The competition was unusually open: most high-scoring solutions depended on
public readers, checkpoints, labels, and discussions. This directory separates
three kinds of evidence:

1. **Leaderboard facts** from the competition’s public submissions.
2. **Solution write-ups** authored by competitors after the event.
3. **Mechanism summaries** written by this archive, with links to originals.

We do not reproduce full third-party posts here. The originals remain on Kaggle;
the summaries preserve the technical claims and the failure conditions that
matter for future competitions.

Files:

- `TOPICS_INDEX.csv`: title, author, votes, URL, and content size for the 22
  captured topics.
- `TOP_WRITEUPS.md`: structured summaries of the strongest solutions.
